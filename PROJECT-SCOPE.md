## 2. Audience & Message

- **Target audience:** anyone wanting a reading; primarily **women aged 27–70**.
- **Competitors:** other astrologers (none named).
- **Core message:** visitors feel *welcome, non‑judged, and leave with more answers than they came with*.
- **Key message:** *"Astrology can help in day‑to‑day life."*
- **Tone:** warm, grounded, practical — not mystical/"woo", not corporate.

## 3. Goals & Conversions

1. **Primary:** Book a reading (appointment).
2. Sell products / readings online (payment).
3. Grow newsletter list (email marketing).
4. Build brand awareness & thought leadership via blog.

Success measured with **Google Analytics**. Target keywords: *astrology, readings*.

## 7. Design Direction — LOCKED

Reference: WhatsApp screenshot ("Aetheris" template) in project folder. Client stated "modern/traditional", "something that grabs attention", no brand guidelines.

### Look & feel
Dark, celestial, editorial‑luxury. Calm and credible.

### Colour
| Role | Value (approx.) |
|---|---|
| Background | Deep navy `#0B1526` |
| Panels / cards | Slightly lighter navy `#0E1B33` |
| Accent | Champagne gold `#D9C08A` |
| Body text | Ivory / off‑white `#F2EEE6` |
| Secondary text | Muted grey‑blue `#9AA5B8` |
| Borders | Gold at ~25% opacity, 1px |

### Typography
- **Display:** elegant serif (Cormorant Garamond / Playfair Display), italic serif for quotes
- **Body / UI:** clean sans (Inter)
- **Eyebrows:** small uppercase, tracked (e.g. "THE PRACTITIONER")
- **Wordmark:** spaced uppercase serif

### Homepage structure (in order)
1. **Nav** — wordmark left, links centre (Home, About, Services, Booking, Blog, Contact), outlined gold **"Book a Reading"** button right
2. **Hero** — centred; small pill tag, large serif headline, one‑line subhead, solid gold primary CTA + outlined secondary CTA; thin gold hairline with star glyph
3. **Services** — heading + subline; 2‑up grid: bordered card (number label, title, description, price, arrow) + image‑backed card (astrolabe/celestial imagery)
4. **About / The Practitioner** — large B&W portrait left with overlapping stat badge ("X+ years"); story copy, italic pull quote, underlined text link right
5. **Testimonials** — three bordered cards: quote mark, italic quote, name + descriptor
6. **Blog / Latest** — 2‑up featured posts with image, date label, serif title, excerpt; "View all" link
7. **Final CTA band** — bordered box, large serif headline, one line, solid gold button
8. **Footer** — wordmark + copyright left, utility links right

### Details
- 1px low‑opacity gold borders instead of shadows; square / near‑square corners
- Generous vertical spacing; full‑bleed dark sections with subtle tonal shifts
- Imagery: monochrome portrait, eclipse, astrolabe, navy still‑life — cinematic; **no cartoon zodiac icons**
- Mobile: single‑column cards, collapsed nav, centred hero
