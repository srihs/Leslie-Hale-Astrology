Questions people ask

What do I need to have ready?

Your date, time and place of birth. If you don't know your exact birth time, get in touch and we'll work with what you have.

How do online readings work?

You'll receive a video link by email. Phone readings are also available.

Can I reschedule?

Yes — up to 24 hours before your session, free of charge.

Is a reading a substitute for professional advice?

No. Readings are for guidance and reflection and are not a substitute for medical, legal or financial advice.
