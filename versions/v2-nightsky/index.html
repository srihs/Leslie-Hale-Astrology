About Leslie

Come as you are. Leave with answers.

I've been reading charts for a long time, and the thing I've learned is that astrology works best when it's practical. Not vague, not doom‑laden — a clear way of looking at your life and its timing.

Nothing you bring to a reading will surprise me or be judged. We'll talk plainly about what's going on and what the sky suggests you do with it.

"Astrology isn't about fate. It's about knowing the weather before you plan the day."
